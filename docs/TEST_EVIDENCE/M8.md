## Known gaps / honest limitations after M8

- Trace theater renders up to 400 children per node and aggregates runs of
  identical siblings (×N); beyond that, records remain accessible via the
  timeline but are not individually drawn (labeled in-UI).
- AST overview defaults to Level 2 semantic zoom for legibility; Full is one
  click away.
- The landing hero is a labeled conceptual animation (no measured-timing claim).
- Demo "Run & replay" intentionally does not auto-analyze; one click on
  "Analyze" opens the static journey (keeps container load predictable).
- Bundle ~460 kB (145 kB gzip) before lazy-loading; code-splitting deferred
  (see REMAINING_M8_M9.md).
- The ZCode in-app browser screenshot pipeline occasionally wedged the tab
  compositor during evidence capture (tool quirk, not a product defect — pages
  stayed responsive and were re-verified in fresh tabs).
